Sale Order Cost Aggregation

Streamline your project margins by consolidating costs into a single primary line, while keeping individual components editable.

Key Features

Cost Aggregation

Automatically sum up all individual component costs and attribute them to the top-most line of your order.

Individual Editability

Total cost is aggregated, but each line's unique cost remains fully editable for granular control.

Clean Margin Logic

Eliminates "negative margin" components from your reports, focusing the profit burden on your primary anchor service.

Why Choose This Customization?

✓
Accounting Safe

Operates within the sales margin layer without affecting stock valuation or general ledger entries.

✓
Dynamic Sorting

Simply drag and drop any line to the top to make it the new cost anchor. The recalculation is immediate.

✓
Report Optimization

Standard Odoo Pivot and Graph views will reflect a consolidated and cleaner project profitability view.
